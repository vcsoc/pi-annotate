09 · High Contrast & Keyboard First

Idea: an accessibility-led variant: pure black/white with yellow focus, 14–16px text, 36–48px targets, every icon paired with a word, and state carried by text/shape (✓, ◉, “Needs note”) rather than colour alone. Visible access keys (C = Capture, S = Send). Settings use labelled fieldsets in two columns so the whole form fits.

Size: 480 × 760. Trade-offs: larger type means fewer drafts visible (≈3 rows); heavier visual weight than the other directions — best offered as a theme that follows the OS “increase contrast” setting.
