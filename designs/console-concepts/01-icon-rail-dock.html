01 · Icon Rail Dock Narrow vertical panel

Idea: a slim tool rail (Capture, Area, Freehand, Drafts, Destination) keeps every action one click away while the remaining 272px column stacks destination, list and the selected draft expanded inline. Settings become an accordion with one-line summaries so nothing scrolls endlessly.

Size: 320 × full work-area height (shown 860px), docked right with a 6px gap. Trade-offs: icon rail needs tooltips/labels to be learnable; the inline expanded draft limits how many rows are visible at once.
