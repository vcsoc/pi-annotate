03 · Filmstrip Drawer Narrow vertical panel

Idea: one annotation at a time gets the full panel width (big screenshot + note), while a 4-column filmstrip below shows the whole batch and its capacity — empty dashed slots make “3 of 8” visible at a glance. All primary actions sit in a thumb-friendly bottom dock. Settings split into three in-panel tabs (Shortcut · Capture · Batch), each fitting without scrolling.

Size: 340 × full work-area height (shown 860px), docked right; a gold edge glow + “Docked ▸” chip show the docked side. Trade-offs: only one note is readable at a time; 12-slot batches need a third filmstrip row.
