02 · Timeline Feed Narrow vertical panel

Idea: annotations read as a chronological feed — newest at the bottom, each with source app, time and a screenshot so the highlighted mark is visible without opening it. A split Capture button carries the mark tool; the send controls live in a sticky bottom bar like a chat composer. Settings use grouped rows with values on the right; editing opens a small inline popover.

Size: 300 × full work-area height (shown 860px), docked left. Trade-offs: wide thumbnails use vertical space (≈4 visible at 8 drafts — older ones collapse to compact rows); the feed metaphor suits ordered batches but makes reordering less obvious.
