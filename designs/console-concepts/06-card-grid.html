06 · Card Grid Board

Idea: treat the batch like a mood board — four-column cards with the full app thumbnail on top and the note beneath, plus an “add” card that also shows remaining capacity. Clicking a card opens a lightbox editor with ‹ › navigation. Settings become a tile dashboard: every option is visible at once, with live summaries.

Size: 640 × 560. Trade-offs: scanning is fast and visual, but notes are truncated to two lines in the grid; editing takes one extra click (lightbox). Drops to three/two columns as the window narrows.
