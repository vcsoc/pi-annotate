04 · Command Pill

Idea: the Console collapses into a 600×56 floating pill — drag grip, Capture, mark tool, a dot meter for the batch, gear, Send — so it barely covers the app you are reviewing. Clicking the meter unfolds a tray with card thumbnails and the selected note. Settings open as an anchored two-column popover (four small groups) instead of a scrolling page.

Size: collapsed 600 × 56; expanded 600 × 440; settings popover 420 × 360 (window grows to fit). Trade-offs: very low footprint, but the destination is hidden in the tray, so a session-name tooltip on Send is essential; not suited to edge docking.
