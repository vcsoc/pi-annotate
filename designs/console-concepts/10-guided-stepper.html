10 · Guided Stepper

Idea: turn the hint line (“Capture → mark → note → save → send”) into the UI itself: a 4-step progress header (Capture · Mark & note · Review · Send). Each step shows only what matters, which suits first-time users and makes the privacy check an explicit step. Settings slide in as a drawer with chip anchors and one-line rows.

Size: 480 × 600. Trade-offs: the most guided and least cluttered, but slower for repeat batches (mitigated: the shortcut and “Capture another” jump straight back to step 1, and steps are clickable).
