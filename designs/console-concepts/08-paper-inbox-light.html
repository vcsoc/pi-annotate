08 · Paper Inbox · light theme

Idea: a calm, light, mail-client look for daytime use: annotations are “messages” in an inbox (dot = has note, amber ring = needs a note), the open one expands in place. Destination and send mode sit in a single “To:” line like an email header. Settings read as one editable sentence — every value is a token you click — so the whole configuration fits in one glance.

Size: 440 × 620. Trade-offs: light UI is easier to read next to light apps but glares in dark desktops (should follow the OS theme); the sentence form is friendly but less scannable for translation/locales.
