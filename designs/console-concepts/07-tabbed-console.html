07 · Tabbed Console

Idea: the closest evolution of today’s window: same size, but three tabs — Annotations (capture + review), Send (destination, mode, privacy check, one big Send) and Settings (sub-tabs General / Capture / Batch). Each tab has room to breathe instead of stacking everything above the list.

Size: 420 × 600 (current default ≈ 400 × 600; min 320 × 360). Trade-offs: sending becomes a deliberate second step (good for the privacy check, one more click for power users — the footer button jumps straight to Send); destination is not visible while capturing, so the footer repeats it.
